Any closed path = a sum of spinning arrows.

A closed curve in four dimensions. Every circle is a spinning ellipse in 4D; you see its 3D “shadow”, viewed along the 4th axis w. Colour is w: blue = near, bright = far. The cube-in-a-cube is a tesseract, for scale.
